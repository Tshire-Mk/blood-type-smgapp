Why knowing your blood type saves lives — especially in rural SA

⚠️ DANGER • EMERGENCY

Not knowing your blood type can kill you in an accident

In trauma, doctors need blood in 10 mins. If you don't know your type, they waste time testing or risk wrong transfusion.

🩸 Source: SANBS • 3.5M trauma cases/year in SA • Tap for more
👶🏾 RURAL CHILDREN • NUTRITION

Rural kids eat same food, but need different iron by blood type

O children need 2x more heme iron (meat) than A children. Same pap diet = anemia for O kids, hidden hunger.

📊 Source: WHO Anemia Report • 61% rural SA kids anemic • Tap
🩸 BLOOD TYPE DIET • SCIENCE

Your blood type changes how you absorb vitamins

A types make less stomach acid → absorb 30% less B12 from meat. O types absorb 2x more iron from red meat. One diet does NOT fit all.

🔬 Source: American Journal of Clinical Nutrition • Tap
🤰🏾 PREGNANCY RISK

Mothers who don't know type risk baby's life

Rh- mothers carrying Rh+ baby need injection within 72hrs. If she doesn't know she's Rh-, baby can die. 15% of SA women are Rh-.

👶🏾 Source: Dept of Health SA • Maternal deaths preventable • Tap
🍽️ HIDDEN HUNGER • SA

We have food, but wrong food for our blood = still malnourished

South Africa wastes R10B food yearly, yet 1 in 4 kids stunted. Eating A-type diet (veg) as O-type (needs meat) = you eat but blood starves.

🇿🇦 Source: StatsSA • Child malnutrition • Tap for solution
